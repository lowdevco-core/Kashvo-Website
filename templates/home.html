FAQ

Questions shops ask.

Does it work with my thermal printer?{% icon "plus" %}

Receipts are laid out for 58 mm and 80 mm paper and print through your browser, so any thermal printer Windows or Android can print to will work.

Is it GST ready?{% icon "plus" %}

Yes. Invoices carry GSTINs, HSN codes and the right split of CGST, SGST or IGST, prices can include or exclude tax, and the GST reports give you GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B summaries.

Do I need to know accounting?{% icon "plus" %}

No. Every bill and payment posts its own entries, so your books balance without you learning double entry.

Can I bring my existing data?{% icon "plus" %}

Yes. Import items and parties from Excel or CSV in a few minutes.
