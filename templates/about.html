About us

Built for the counter.

Kashvo is billing, POS and accounting for the shops that keep India running: the kirana down the road, the wholesaler on market street, the service shop with a queue at the door.

Our story

One app instead of four.

We kept seeing shop owners juggle a billing app, a separate POS, a spreadsheet for stock and a folder of receipts for the accountant. None of them agreed at the end of the month.

So we built one app where the bill is the accounting entry. Sell at the counter, and stock, GST and the books update together.

Most billing software is built for accountants. We build for the person at the counter with a queue in front of them.
