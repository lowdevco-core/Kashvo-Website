Wholesale billing looks nothing like a retail counter. Bills are larger, buyers are regular, prices depend on quantity and relationship, and most sales are on credit. Software built for retail checkout often struggles here. These are the features that matter for traders and distributors.

1. Prices that change with quantity

A carton costs less per piece than a single piece. Your billing software should let you set a wholesale price that applies automatically once the quantity crosses a level, for example ₹42 per piece normally and ₹38 per piece from 24 pieces. The salesperson shouldn't have to remember or calculate it.

2. Party-specific rates and discounts

Big buyers negotiate their own rates. Whether that's a fixed discount percentage or special prices on certain items, it should be stored with the party and applied on every bill. That stops both under-charging and awkward calls when a buyer spots the wrong rate.

3. Credit limits that actually stop over-exposure

In wholesale, one large unpaid account can wipe out a month's margin. Set a credit limit for each buyer and have the software warn you before a bill takes them over it. Pair this with clear payment terms on every invoice. More in managing customer credit.

4. Estimates and orders that become invoices

Buyers often ask for a quotation first, then confirm an order, then take delivery in parts. Look for estimates and sales orders that convert to an invoice in one click, so nothing is typed twice and quantities carry over exactly.

5. Delivery challans

When goods move before the final invoice, for example goods on approval or partial deliveries, a delivery challan records the movement. Converting challans into invoices later keeps stock and billing aligned.

6. Interstate sales done right

Wholesalers sell across state borders far more often than retailers. The software must charge IGST automatically when the place of supply is another state, and record the buyer's GSTIN and state correctly. See CGST, SGST and IGST explained.

7. Statements and settlements

Buyers pay in lumps against many invoices. Record each payment against the bills it settles, and send a clean statement showing bills, payments and balance. It's the single best tool for getting paid on time.

8. Purchase side and margins

Wholesale margins are thin. Knowing your last purchase price, the supplier you bought from, and the margin on every sale helps you price confidently. Supply-side features such as purchase orders and reorder levels keep fast movers in stock without overbuying.
